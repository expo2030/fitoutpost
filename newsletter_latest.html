Editor's Brief

Germany's fit-out sector is consolidating momentum around flexible workspace and residential refurbishment, with major contractors including Morgan Sindall Group expanding operations in the region. Simultaneously, Southeast Asia — particularly Malaysia and Oman — is witnessing accelerating investment in hospitality and commercial development, driven by tourism infrastructure and favourable financing conditions. The divergence underscores a broader pattern: mature markets favour adaptive reuse and workplace transformation, whilst emerging economies prioritise new-build hospitality and mixed-use development.

Capital deployment remains heavily skewed toward hospitality pipelines across Africa, the Middle East, and the Caribbean, with Morocco, Egypt, and Oman leading regional growth. Australia's hotel sector is particularly active, though office refurbishment remains concentrated in Western markets. France's Eurosatory partnerships and Italy's cultural preservation projects indicate growing client appetite for specialised, high-specification fit-out work — suggesting margin potential for contractors with heritage expertise.

Tender activity remains modest, though Ireland's healthcare and retail sectors present immediate procurement opportunities. The UK's Cardiff office tender and Italy's RAI engineering services contract suggest steady institutional demand. Notably, no major contract awards have yet signalled pricing direction for H2 2026, leaving cost inflation narratives unvalidated by recent procurement outcomes.

As AI tools reshape workplace design baselines and cost expectations shift, the critical question for fit-out specialists is whether emerging-market tourism growth can sustain margins whilst mature markets demand increasingly bespoke, tech-integrated solutions at compressed budgets.
